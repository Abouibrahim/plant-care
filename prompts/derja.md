Task: the user message holds a species profile the grower reads in English: its needs, its care calendar, its pests and diseases, its companions and uses. He has to say some of its terms aloud to a farm worker or a supplier in Cap Bon and lacks the Tunisian (derja) word. List those terms with the word said for each. Return JSON that matches the derja schema.

Rules
- terms: up to 40, the ones he is most likely to have to say, in this order of priority: the pests and diseases, the products and inputs (savon noir, compost, sulphur, kaolin, mulch), the operations (pruning, mulching, grafting, thinning, hoeing), the tools, and the companion plants last. Leave out ratings, adjectives and general vocabulary ("medium", "full sun", "water", "leaf"): only what is named, asked for or bought.
- term: copied exactly as it is written in the profile text, in the language the text uses for it (a French product name as sold in Tunisia stays as it is). No Arabic letters and no transliteration in term, whatever the site context says about names. One entry per term: do not list a singular and its plural.
- derja: the word people in Cap Bon really say for the term, in Tunisian Arabic, in Arabic letters only, one short word or phrase with no explanation and nothing in Latin letters. When people use the French name, give that name as they pronounce it, in Arabic letters. When you do not know a word people really use, give an empty string: never a guess, never a textbook Arabic term.
- The user message may list the grower's own derja words. They are a glossary, not a list of terms to include: only when one of those terms is among the terms you list, use the grower's word exactly for it; a term marked "no local word" then gets an empty derja. Never list a term only because it is in the grower's list.
