# Phase 14 — Robustness: the round without signal, no paid answer lost, the run on record

Done when, on the deployed Vercel URL, the Today screen opens on its last list without a connection and takes a Done or a Skip that is sent when the signal comes back; a question, a check or a diagnosis whose reply the phone loses is found again instead of paid for twice; Settings says when the morning run last ran and what it did, and Today reads its list again as soon as that run has happened; Settings shows what the Claude calls cost this month; and the morning notifications and the notes the server writes follow the language chosen in Settings.

Reference: the review of 2026-10-30 (`docs/REVIEW-2026-10-30.md`), whose suggested Phase 11 was its items 3.5, 2.1, 2.3, 5.3 and 4.2, left open while Phases 11 to 13 built the batches and the kinds of work. Mohamed chose it on 2026-11-08 ("Start robustness phase"). Three critics read the first design against the code (a grower at the farm at 06:30, the edge cases, the project's rules); their points changed it as noted below, and `docs/DECISIONS.md` (2026-11-08) gives every decision with its reason, the earlier decisions it supersedes included.

## Plan

### Today without a connection (review 3.5)

- **The last list is kept on the phone.** Each time Today reads its list, the tasks of the week, the day's done count and the day it was read are written to `localStorage` (`plantcare.today`, a few KB to a few hundred). Today renders that copy at once, before the fresh read, and replaces it when the read lands. When the read fails, the copy stays on screen with its note.
  - **Read against the phone's day.** The copy is re-dated when it is recalled: `today` is the phone's date, so a watering due yesterday reads as late, the week strip starts today, the folds and the Choose ticks compare to today, and the midnight tick does not loop; the done count is the copy's day's and reads 0 on another day. (The critics: a copy from last evening rendered under yesterday's date at 06:30.)
  - **One banner, not three.** Offline, Today shows one line: "Offline · shown as of Tue 19:12 · 2 actions waiting", with Retry and Send now; a network failure reads as "Network error…", never the browser's wording; the refresh note stays out while offline.
  - **The weather card** keeps its last rows the same way (`plantcare.weather`) and shows them when its read fails. **The badge** counts the kept copy (late or due today, less the actions waiting) when its own count fails, so an app opened without signal is not blank.
- **Done and Skip work without a connection.** A Done or a Skip (one card, a batch, Done-all) made while the phone says it is offline, or while the write fails for lack of a connection or hangs past 8 s, is kept in a queue on the phone (`plantcare.queue`): the card ticks and folds as it does now, the round moves, and the toast reads "Done: Water OL-01 · saved on the phone" with Undo. Each entry holds the kind, the ids, the note or reason, and the tap's time, which goes on the rows as `acted_at` when the entry is sent; a single card is sent through the single write (`completeTask`, `skipTask`), a set through the set write, so the journal reads as it does online.
  - **Applied at display, never baked in.** The kept copy stays the list as read; the queue is applied whenever a list is shown, kept or fresh: the queued ids leave, the queued Dones count in the round. So a fresh read that lands while an entry is still waiting or in flight brings nothing back, and Undo of a waiting entry only takes it out of the queue.
  - **Sent with a session, serially.** The queue is sent when the connection returns (`online`), when the app comes back to the foreground, when Today opens, and on Send now, one send at a time behind one promise; and only once `supabase.auth.getSession()` returns a session, since a write with no token goes out under the anon key, matches no row, and would read as "closed meanwhile". Each write has the 8 s bound; a failure keeps the entry, with its attempt counted, and the banner says the last error once the phone is online.
  - **A row closed meanwhile.** An entry whose write changes no row is not dropped in silence. The rows are read back: one the server closed (skipped with the rain note, "Window ended", "Superseded…" or "Missed watering", in either language) is closed as the grower asked, since the tap came first and the tree was watered; one still pending means the write never applied (no token yet, a bad moment), so the entry stays; one the grower closed from another screen is left as it is, and the banner says "1 saved action was not applied: the task had been closed meanwhile", with the list read again.
  - **Undo.** On a waiting entry, Undo removes it and the cards come back from the copy, a batch's unticked plants with them. On an entry being sent, Undo waits for the send and reopens the ids it closed, as the online Undo does. On an entry sent, Undo is a reopen, online. The toast lasts its five seconds as before.
  - **The queue survives a local sign-out** and is sent after the next sign-in; it belongs to the phone, not the session.
- **What stays online.** Snooze (several writes, and a slot search) says "Snooze needs a connection" offline or after the 8 s bound; the expansion and the Claude calls fail as they do now; the Undo of an action already sent is a reopen. A Done made offline on a plant's Calendar tab is not queued: the queue is Today's.
- **The sign-in holds offline.** The app shell shows when the stored sign-in cannot be refreshed for lack of a connection (an app opened at the farm the morning after its hourly token ran out would otherwise show Sign in after a 25 s wait): the auth layer keeps an `offline` state while storage holds a session and the refresh fails on the network, the kept copy renders with its note, and writes and the queue wait for the session, which comes back with the signal. A refresh the server refuses still signs out.
- **Why localStorage.** The copy is small and read once at mount, where a synchronous read gives the list without a spinner; IndexedDB would add a frame of "Loading…" for nothing.
- **Reading a failure.** `src/lib/errors.ts` gathers `isNetworkFailure` (the browser's own words: failed to fetch, network error, load failed, aborted, timed out) from the two copies the screens had; a bare TypeError no longer counts, so a programming error is never queued as "no connection".

### No paid answer lost (review 2.1)

- **The app names the row before the call.** Ask sends `id`, `thread_id` and `asked_at` with the question; Check sends `id` and `checked_at`; Diagnose sends the planned photo row (`id`, `storage_path` under its own plant, `taken_at`) with the images. Each route validates them (UUIDs, an ISO instant, a path of the form `<plant_id>/<ms>.jpg`), and once Claude has answered writes the row itself with the service role (`questions`, `planting_checks`, or `photos` with the diagnosis in `ai_result`), under a 5 s bound and before the ledger, then replies. The app's own save then meets the row: `insertQuestion` and `insertCheck` read the unique id (23505) as saved; `insertPhoto` upserts on the id and overwrites the route's row with the same content. The route's write failing never fails the paid call: the app's save then writes the row, or says the table is missing, as today. Route-written rows keep Claude's answer as it came, with the usage; every reader already normalises on read (`storedAnswer`, `storedCheck`, `problemResult`).
- **A lost reply is looked for, not paid twice.** When the call ends in a network error (status 0), the app asks the table for the row by its id at once, then every 10 s until 300 s after sending, each read bounded to 10 s, as `api.profile` does with `lookup_only`. The panel says "The connection dropped while Claude was answering. Checking whether the answer has arrived…"; the row found is the answer, shown and listed in the journal. A table that does not exist ends the asking at once (the route could not have written either). When nothing arrives by then: "No answer was found. The call may have been declined or lost; asking again is a new call." (A refusal is not saved by either side, so it leaves nothing to find.)
- **The panel is not a trap.** While the app only polls the table, Close and the back gesture work again (only Send stays off): leaving stops the asking, and the row, if the function finished, is in the journal at the next reload. Only the first call keeps the sheet closed, as today.
- **Diagnose's photo.** The path and the id are planned before the call; the upload of the photo and its thumbnail starts together with the call, not before it, so a weak link is not crossed twice in a row and a failed upload does not stop a diagnosis that would have succeeded. The route writes the row with the planned path whatever the upload did; the app's save after the answer, or after the recovery, finishes or repeats the upload (Save again already uploads over its planned path). On a refusal or an answered failure (a 4xx or 5xx) the object is removed again once its upload has settled, best effort; so is it when a recovery ends with no row. The follow-up reminder stays the app's, written after the answer or the recovery; a diagnosis recovered by the journal alone offers "Set the reminder" on its card, as a failed task write does today.
- **Older builds.** The ids are optional in the requests: an app not yet reloaded gets the old behaviour.
- **Decisions superseded, with their reason.** Phase 4's "the photo is saved after Claude answers, so a failed or refused call leaves nothing in Storage" and Phases 6 and 7's "the route only reads; the app saves": a paid answer lost on mobile data costs more than an orphaned object, which the removal covers in every case but a function that dies after a lost reply, and `deletePlant` already removes a plant's folder.
- **Not here.** A regeneration whose reply is lost (the review's 2.1 did not list it); `Save again` keeps its meaning for a failed app-side save.

### The morning run on record (review 2.3)

- **The run writes what it did.** A real run (never a dry run) writes the `settings` row `daily:started` (`at`, the Tunis date) as it begins, and `daily:last` as soon as its data is final, before the pushes go out: `at`, the date it ran for, the tasks created, removed, expired and superseded, the waterings the rain skipped, the weather error if any; the pushes sent, failed and the subscriptions they went to are added with a second small write that keeps `at`. A run that fails writes `at`, the date and the error. The settings table missing never fails the run, and a record that could not be written is reported in the response (`record_error`).
- **Today follows the record, by its stamp.** Today reads the record with its list and keeps its `at`. From 06:05, every five minutes while the screen is visible, and on each return to the foreground, it reads the record again; a record with another `at`, or one for today where there was none, means a run came after the list, and the list, the weather and the badge are read again. Clocks are never compared: the phone's may be minutes off Vercel's. The looks stop once a record for today has been seen, or at 07:15 (a run started at 06:59 can end at 07:04 under the 300 s limit). When the record cannot be read at all (the settings table missing), the first look of the day reads the list anyway, as the fixed 06:05 re-read did until now. A push reaching the open app still reads at once (Phase 13); since the record now precedes the pushes, that read sees it.
- **Settings says it.** The Weather card gains the line: "Last morning run: Tue 4 Nov, 06:41 · 3 tasks added, 2 waterings skipped after rain, 6 tasks tidied, 3 notifications to 1 phone", or "… failed on Tue 4 Nov, 06:41: …", or "No morning run recorded yet"; "no phone has reminders on" when there was no subscription. A record from an earlier day comes with "No run yet today" before 07:15 and, after it, with a warning that none was recorded today; a start marker for today with no record reads "started 06:41, not finished". The settings table missing shows the Phase 5 instruction, not "no run".

### What the Claude calls cost (review 5.3)

- **A ledger per month, failures included.** `callJson` takes an `onUsage` callback and calls it once per call with the tokens it spent and the outcome: answered, refused, or failed after spending (a cut output after the doubled retry, no text, unreadable JSON). Each route passes `recordUsage`, which adds the call to the `settings` row `usage:YYYY-MM` of the Tunis month: calls, failed calls, calls a fallback model answered, input, output, thinking, cache read and cache write tokens, in all and per route. The profile counts only when Claude was called (a lookup is free). The write is bounded to 5 s, runs after the answer's own row and before the reply, and never fails the paid call.
- **Two calls ending together.** The row is read with its `updated_at` and written back only where it is still the one read (the trigger keeps `updated_at`), with up to two more tries when it was not; so two routes ending in the same moment lose nothing. No SQL: the trigger exists since Phase 5.
- **Settings shows it.** A "Claude usage" card: this month's calls and an estimate at list prices, "November 2026: 23 calls, about $2.10", the calls by kind beneath, the tokens in and out (thinking inside the out), the failed calls and those a fallback model answered (counted at Fable prices), and last month's line. The prices sit in one constant (`src/lib/usage.ts`: Fable 5.1 at $10 per million input tokens, $50 per million output, $0.25 per million cache reads, $12.50 per million cache writes, as published on 2026-11-08) and are printed on the card, so a wrong figure is visible. The estimate is for Claude alone; Vercel and Supabase stay on their free tiers.

### The notifications in French (review 4.2)

- **The choice reaches the server, and is seen to.** Settings → Language keeps storing the choice on the phone and reloading at once, as Phase 5 decided, so the switch still works offline. The `settings` row `lang` is written beside it, bounded to 5 s, and again whenever the app starts online with a stored choice that differs from the row, so a write cut by the reload or made offline heals itself. The card reads the row back: "Notifications: Français", or "The choice has not reached the server yet". Without a stored choice (the phone's language decides the screens), the server stays English, as before.
- **What the server composes in that language.** The morning run reads the row and composes the digest, the weather push, the rain note and the notes the expansion writes in it, with its dates in that language too ("À partir du 1 nov."); so does the app's own expansion (`/api/expand`) for its notes. Any failed read gives English and never fails the run; the dry run's digest shows the language in use.
- **The expansion reads both languages.** A window the expansion closed is recognised by its note in either language (`carriedOver`), so a switch of language never brings a finished window back.
- **Unchanged.** The Claude-written text (profiles, the task titles copied from them, identifications) and the diagnosis stay in the language they were written in; the planting check follows the app language as before; the functions' own error texts, and the record's errors, stay English literals.

## Acceptance tests (run on the phone, installed app on 0.14.0)

1. **Today without signal, the farm morning.** Use the app online in the evening, and end with a Done. The next morning, in airplane mode, open the app from the home screen: within a few seconds the list shows under today's date, overdue waterings marked late, the round at 0 done, with the banner "Offline · shown as of Mon 19:12", and the task done in the evening is not among them; the weather card shows its last rows; the Today badge counts the due tasks. Turn airplane mode off, tap Retry: the banner goes and the list is fresh.
2. **Done offline.** In airplane mode, Done on a watering and Skip on another task: both cards leave, the round counts the Done, the toasts read "Done: … · saved on the phone" and "Skipped: … · saved on the phone", and the banner says "2 actions waiting". Tap Undo on the second toast: the card comes back and the banner counts 1. Turn airplane mode off: within a few seconds the banner goes; the plant's journal shows the Done at the time of the tap.
3. **Done-all and a batch offline.** In airplane mode, Done-all on the waterings and Done on a window batch with a note: one toast each, the banner counts 2 actions; back online, both land, the note on every ticked plant.
4. **A row closed meanwhile.** In airplane mode, Done on a watering, then from a computer skip that watering with a reason of your own; back online, the banner says "1 saved action was not applied: the task had been closed meanwhile" and the list shows the skip. Then the rain case when it comes: a Done tapped offline before 06:00 on a watering the run skips after rain lands as done.
5. **Snooze offline.** Snooze in airplane mode: "Snooze needs a connection", the card stays.
6. **Weak signal.** With one bar, Done on a task: within about 8 s the card has either landed or the toast says it is saved on the phone; nothing spins for minutes.
7. **A lost Ask reply.** Ask Claude a question and switch to airplane mode while "Claude is thinking…" shows (within the first 20 s). The panel says the connection dropped and it is checking, and Close works; turn airplane mode off: within about a minute the answer shows, with its usage, and the journal lists it once. Repeat and leave the panel after 30 s: the answer appears in the journal by itself. (Costs two questions.)
8. **A lost check.** The same with Check before planting on a species already in the library: the verdict shows after the signal returns, saved once. (Costs one check.)
9. **A lost diagnosis.** The same with Diagnose: the photo appears in the journal with its diagnosis once, and the follow-up reminder is set. (Costs one diagnosis.) A refusal, if one happens, leaves no photo in the journal and no file in Storage.
10. **The run record.** Settings → Weather shows "Last morning run: …" with the counts after the next 06:00 run; before it, "No run yet today" with the last one. The morning after, Today left open overnight shows the run's result within five minutes of the run, without a tap; at 07:20 on a morning the run did not fire, the card warns.
11. **The ledger.** After tests 7 to 9, Settings → Claude usage counts the calls this month, by kind, with the estimate and the prices; the figure moves after each paid call.
12. **French notifications.** Settings → Language → Français; the card reads "Notifications : Français"; the next morning's notifications read "Ferme : 6 tâches", with "À partir du 1 nov." on an early notice, and a window the expansion closes that day carries "Fenêtre terminée" in the journal. Back to English: the morning after, English again.
13. **The dry run (optional).** `GET /api/daily?dry=1` with the cron secret: the response's digest is in the chosen language, and Settings' "Last morning run" does not change.

## Found in review of the build

Four reviewers read the build (the round without a connection, the lost replies, the server's record, ledger and language, the project's rules) and raised 61 points; a skeptic confirmed two before the session's usage limit cut the verification short, and the other 59 were judged against the code and the browser check by hand. Six were one gap, the first below. What changed:

- **A write cut by its bound was not kept.** `isNetworkFailure` did not know the browser's abort wording ("TimeoutError: signal timed out"), so a Done that hung past 8 s showed "The task could not be updated: TimeoutError…" instead of "saved on the phone", a recovery read that hit its 10 s bound ended the asking, and a bare TypeError still counted as "no connection". Fixed in `src/lib/errors.ts`, tested; `tests/mockdb.ts` now answers an aborted signal as postgrest-js does, and `tests/db.test.ts` covers `actedAt`, the bound, `readTaskStates` and `overrideServerClose`.
- **The kept copy went stale after an online Done.** `keepToday` ran only in the fresh read, so a task done online in the evening came back, late, in the airplane-mode morning, and a second tap on it ended in "not applied: closed meanwhile". The copy now follows the list as shown (kept whenever it changes, under the stamps of the read it came from), and a round of sending takes the rows it closed off the list on screen at once, the round moving with them, instead of letting them reappear until the fresh read.
- **Without a session the reads went out under the anon key.** With the offline shell (a stored sign-in the server could not refresh), a Retry as the signal returned read empty lists under the policies, overwrote the copy with nothing, set the badge to 0 and showed "not the owner" (auth-js keeps a failed refresh for 60 s). Today, the weather card, the badge and the owner check make no read without a session now, and read again when it returns; `AuthProvider` shows the shell at once when the phone starts offline, instead of a spinner for the 25 s auth-js retries the refresh, and a refresh the server refuses (`SIGNED_OUT`) ends the offline shell so Login shows.
- **The banner followed `online` only.** Going offline did not re-render, so "Offline" appeared only at the next tap; when the signal returned with nothing queued, the kept list was not read again. Both events re-render now, and a kept list is read again on `online`.
- **A tap whose write landed but lost its reply was reported as "closed meanwhile".** The online writes carry the tap's time as `acted_at` now, as the queue's do, and the read-back counts a row closed as asked at that time as this action's own write: sent, not left out. A set action whose read-back fails keeps only the ids its write did not close.
- **Snooze had no bound and was attempted offline.** `snoozeTask` takes the signal for each of its writes, and offline it is refused at once with "Snooze needs a connection", as the plan said.
- **Leaving a panel aborted the asking.** The row the function then wrote stayed invisible until the next reload, and the grower asked again. The asking goes on after Close or the back gesture: the answer is saved and `onChanged` reloads the journal or the Health tab (the diagnosis with its reminder written); the Close warnings say what is still at stake. The plan's "leaving stops the asking" is superseded.
- **The ledger was written before the route's row.** `callJson` awaited the ledger write before returning, so the row a lost reply is found by waited on it (up to 15 s after a call that used its 280 s). An answered call's ledger write comes back as `recorded` on the result, and the route awaits it after its own row, before replying.
- **The Language card raced the start-up write** and read "not reached the server" right after the reload; it now reads after `syncLangWithServer`, which runs once per app session. On a phone with no choice of its own it shows the language another phone chose, instead of "English until chosen".
- **The record's fallback never fired.** `loadDailyRecord` turned a missing settings table into "no record", so Today thought the record readable and the first-look-of-the-day read never ran. It throws `SettingsUnavailable` now, which Today reads as unreadable; the Settings card already shows the Phase 5 instruction then.
- **Smaller points.** A run that fails after its data record was written keeps the counts and adds the error; `fallback` counts a call only when the fallback model answered, not a chain refusal or a failure under it; a failed rain skip reads "the rain skip failed", not "the weather could not be fetched"; a queued Done counts in the round only on the day it was tapped; the card finishes its tick before "saved on the phone"; the Undo of a waiting action and a new queued action recount the badge; a queue the phone's storage refuses says so instead of pretending; the send failure line shows only once online, in the app's words; "Affiché tel qu'à 07:12" and the fallback line in both languages; `isInstant` accepts a camera clock up to a year ahead, where a day refused every Send from such a camera; the `ProfileOptions` alias, a constant between imports, stale comments in `en.ts`, `fr.ts` and `api/_usage.ts`, the test of the deadline's own error (`LostReplyError`), the guide's badge sentence, and this file's build list.
- **Left as they are, on purpose.** The recovery also starts when the phone had no signal at Send and the request never left: five minutes of looking for a question never asked, with Close free, since nothing tells that case from a request that left. A row the read-back does not return (the plant deleted) ends the action rather than keeping it. The dates on the Settings card follow the phone's locale, as every date in the app does. A Diagnose sheet left while its upload had failed leaves a row whose photo loads only after Save again.

## Usage check

No new Claude call. The routes gain two small writes per call (the row and the ledger, each bounded); the morning run three more writes (the start marker, the record, the push counts) and one more read (the language); Today one more small read with its list (the record) and, in the cron's hour, one every five minutes while visible until the run is seen; the app one read of the `lang` row at start.
